== Synopsis
[verse]
// tag::synopsis[]
aerospace group [-h|--help] [--window-id <window-id>] (toggle|next|prev|remove|join-left|join-down|join-up|join-right)

// end::synopsis[]

== Description
// tag::body[]
A group occupies one tile and displays its most recently focused member.
The group bar lists members in order. Click a tab or use keyboard commands to
select its member. Only the bar receives clicks; borders remain click-through.

`toggle` creates a group around the target tiled window, or extracts only that window
from its existing group. The remaining members stay grouped.
New tiled windows join the most recently focused tiled group.
`next` and `prev` select members in order and wrap around.
`remove` extracts the target member into a separate tile.
`join-left`, `join-down`, `join-up`, and `join-right` merge the target's group
(or its single window) into the nearest neighboring tile's group.

Groups survive single-member normalization and retain their active member in the
closed-window cache used to restore windows after sleep. Closing the active member
selects the most recently focused remaining member.

Directional `move` moves the whole group, swaps with neighboring tiles, and stops
beside groups in nested splits. With dwindle enabled, movement stops at workspace
edges by default; explicit `--boundaries-action` options override this.
Workspace and monitor transfer, floating, and mouse movement operate on the individual member.
Use `group join-*` or dragging to join. Directional focus skips inactive members. Resize operates
on the group's tile. `flatten-workspace-tree` dissolves groups in that workspace.
In dwindle mode, dropping a tiled or floating window in a group's center or on its
bar joins it on mouse release. Dropping near an edge creates a separate split on
that side; this also extracts a member dropped near its own group's edge.
Outside dwindle mode, dropping anywhere on a group joins it. Animations are not implemented.
